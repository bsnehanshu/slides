Orientation

What's a Game Day?

A collaborative, hands-on learning exercise. You implement AWS + Datadog solutions to solve real-world problems in a gamified, risk-free environment. Unlike a workshop, quests are open-ended — each team figures out the best way forward, there's no single "correct" click-path. Be curious. Try things. You can't break anything real.

Mechanics

Getting logged in

1
Get your OTP
Go to the Game Day URL, enter your email, grab the one-time code.
2
Enter team number + password
On the Team Dashboard, use the team_number and team_password (handed out on paper).
3
Set your team name
Name your team and log into your AWS accounts.
4
Open your site
Your gameday_url is a CloudFront link to the website front-end you'll defend.
5
Get approved
An operator approves your team name; only then do you appear on the scoreboard.
05 / 11
Mechanics

Confirm your Datadog access

The path to check
  • In Datadog, click Cloud → AWS
  • Hit the "Explore your logs" button
  • Seeing logs? You're good to go.
Under the hood
  • CloudWatch metrics stream from your AWS account into Datadog via Kinesis
  • Expect a short lag early on
  • No data yet? Wait a couple of minutes.
06 / 11
MCP / AI quests

Get set up in Kiro

1
Open Cloud IDE (beta) — In the side panel, then click Open Kiro
2
Sign in — Your Organization → Sign in via IAM Identity Center, paste the Start URL
3
Authenticate — KeyCloak if prompted, and click Allow access
4
Open the project — Open the GameDay project folder and trust the authors
5
Confirm connection — Start a chat and confirm Kiro is connected to your Datadog account
⚠ Do NOT edit mcp.json
The MCPs are pre-configured. If Kiro can't connect, wait a moment and retry; still stuck, flag a staffer. Ignore the "too many MCP tools" warning — it's intentional.

How scoring works

Points scale with traffic as the load curve ramps. It's not just brute-force scaling — running lean matters: over-provisioning costs you points. There are easter eggs (including one crypto-scam rabbit hole that pays off if you're brave). Bonus points for finishing quests fast.
